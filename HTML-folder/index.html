Do I need to be a good writer to get started?

+

Not at all. Nextline gives you guidance, examples, and clean layouts so your experience speaks for itself without needing fancy copywriting.

Can I bring my existing CV?

+

Yes, you can easily upload or reference your existing CV data, and our platform will help you reformat and enhance it with modern professional standards.

What makes a CV ATS-friendly?

+

An ATS, or applicant tracking system, helps employers organize applications. Clear headings, a sensible reading order and selectable text make a CV easier to parse.

Can I change templates later?

+

You can switch between different professional templates at any time with a single click without losing your content.

Will AI write my whole CV for me?

+

AI helps you draft bullet points, refine summaries, and optimize phrasing, but you always have full control to customize and review every section.

What can I do for free?

+

You can build, edit, and preview your CV using standard templates and basic features completely for free before deciding to upgrade.
