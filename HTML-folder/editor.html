Dear Jamie,

I'm drawn to Forma's focus on making complex work feel simple. At Harbour Studio, I've worked on that same challenge: turning a complicated setup process into a clearer first-project experience.

My approach starts with listening. I led usability sessions with 12 customers to understand where they were getting stuck, then worked with product managers and engineers to prioritise the changes that mattered. I enjoy connecting those research findings to practical, accessible interface patterns.

Before Harbour Studio, I designed customer journeys and prototypes at North & Co. That work taught me how to make decisions collaboratively and communicate the thinking behind a design—not just the final screens.

I'd welcome the chance to bring that approach to Forma and learn more about the problems your team is working on. Thank you for considering my application.
